Topic: Human Anatomy - The Heart

Circulatory Paths

The heart functions as a double pump:
Pulmonary: Deoxygenated blood to the lungs.
Systemic: Oxygenated blood to the rest of the body.

Cardiac Components

  • Four Chambers: Two Atria and Two Ventricles.
  • Valves ensure one-way blood flow.
  • Coronary arteries supply the heart muscle itself.
