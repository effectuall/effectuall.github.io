Topic: Single Displacement Reaction (Zn + H₂SO₄)

Chemical Equation

Zn(s) + H₂SO₄(aq) → ZnSO₄(aq) + H₂(g)↑

Observations

  • Evolution of colorless, odorless bubbles (Hydrogen).
  • Exothermic reaction (test tube gets warm).
  • Zinc granules gradually disappear.
